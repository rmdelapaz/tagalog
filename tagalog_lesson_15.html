Mga Kulay, Panahon at Damit

Colors, weather, and clothing — describing the world around you

With your grammar toolkit in place, it's time to add the everyday words that make conversations come alive. This lesson gives you three practical bundles — colors, weather, and clothing — so you can describe what you see, comment on the day, and talk about what people are wearing. These are pure vocabulary you'll use constantly.

1. Mga Kulay — Colors

TagalogEnglish
pulared
asulblue
dilawyellow
berdegreen
putiwhite
itimblack
kahelorange
kulay-rosaspink
  • Anong kulay ito? — Pula ito. (What color is this? — It's red.)
  • Asul ang langit. (The sky is blue.)
  • Gusto ko ang berdeng damit. (I like the green dress.) — note the linker -ng
Tip: to say "the color ___," you can put kulay in front — kulay pula (red-colored), kulay asul (blue-colored). Handy when a color has no single short word.

2. Ang Panahon — Weather

Many weather words are just ma- adjectives (Lesson 7) built on a noun — araw (sun) → maaraw (sunny):

TagalogEnglish
mainithot
malamigcold / cool
maarawsunny
maulanrainy
umuulanit's raining
bagyostorm / typhoon
tag-inithot/dry season
tag-ulanrainy season
  • Kumusta ang panahon? — Mainit ngayon. (How's the weather? — It's hot today.)
  • Umuulan, magdala ka ng payong. (It's raining, bring an umbrella.)
  • May bagyo daw bukas. (There's a storm tomorrow, they say.)
Two seasons, not four: the Philippines has tag-init (the hot, dry season) and tag-ulan (the rainy season) — so weather talk usually centers on heat and rain.

3. Mga Damit — Clothing

TagalogEnglish
damitclothes / dress
kamisetashirt / t-shirt
pantalonpants / trousers
sapatosshoes
medyassocks
sombrerohat
bestidadress

  • Bago ang sapatos ko. (My shoes are new.)
  • Ano ang suot mo? (What are you wearing?) — suot = worn/wearing
  • Suot niya ang pulang kamiseta. (He's wearing the red shirt.)

Putting It Together

Notice how these bundles combine with grammar you already know — colors describe clothing with the linker, and weather uses ma- adjectives and the may from Lesson 10:

  • Gusto ko ng asul na pantalon. (I want blue pants.)
  • Malamig ngayon, magsuot ka ng jacket. (It's cold today, wear a jacket.)
  • May puting sombrero siya. (She has a white hat.)

Practice — Describe It

Exercise 1: Name the color

Fill in the Tagalog color:

  • "The sky is blue." → "_____ ang langit." (Answer: asul)
  • "It's red." → "_____ ito." (Answer: pula)
  • "I like the green dress." → "Gusto ko ang _____ng damit." (Answer: berde)

Exercise 2: What's the weather?

Fill with mainit, malamig, or umuulan:

  • "It's hot today." → "_____ ngayon." (Answer: mainit)
  • "It's raining." → "_____." (Answer: umuulan)
  • "It's cold, wear a jacket." → "_____, magsuot ka ng jacket." (Answer: malamig)

Exercise 3: Clothing

Fill in the clothing word:

  • "My shoes are new." → "Bago ang _____ ko." (Answer: sapatos)
  • "He's wearing a red shirt." → "Suot niya ang pulang _____." (Answer: kamiseta)

Why This Lesson Matters

Colors, weather, and clothing are the small-talk backbone — you'll comment on the rain, ask a price for the blue shirt, describe someone in a crowd. These everyday words plug straight into the grammar you've built, so each one instantly becomes dozens of sentences.
