🎉 All 24 lessons are live — the complete B1–B2 intermediate course.

You've built a complete A1–A2 foundation: pronunciation, core grammar, the focus system, verbs, modals, negation, questions, requests, and everyday topics. This intermediate course takes you the rest of the way — the full verb-affix system, complex sentences, conditionals, opinions and argument, storytelling, register, and the language of media and abstract topics.

It's built as a two-tier series: B1 (Intermediate) first, then B2 (Upper-Intermediate). Each lesson keeps the same learning tools you know — objectives, audio, a self-check quiz, interactive exercises, a journal, and progress tracking — with its own glossary, Anki deck, and can-do checklist.
