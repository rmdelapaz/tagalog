Sanhi, Bunga at Pagsalungat — Cause, Result & Concession

The formal connectors that carry an argument: because of this, as a result, despite, nevertheless

In Lesson 8 you learned everyday connectors — kasi, kaya, pero. B2 gives you the formal, discourse-level versions that essays, editorials, and serious discussion run on. They let you signal cause, draw a conclusion, and — the mark of a real arguer — concede a point while holding your own.

1. Leveling Up from kasi / kaya

Your casual toolkit maps directly onto a formal one. Same relationships, weightier words:

RelationshipCasual (Lesson 8)Formal (this lesson)
causekasidahil sa, dahil dito, bunga ng
result / conclusionkayakaya naman, samakatuwid, sa gayon
contrast / concessionpero, kahitgayunpaman, sa kabila ng, bagama't

2. Cause: dahil sa, dahil dito, bunga ng

To state a reason formally:

ConnectorMeaningExample
dahil sabecause of (+ noun)Dahil sa ulan, naantala ang biyahe. — Because of the rain, the trip was delayed.
dahil ditobecause of this…dahil dito, kailangan nating kumilos. — …because of this, we must act.
bunga ng / dulot ngas a result of / brought about byBunga ng polusyon ang sakit. — The illness is a result of pollution.

3. Result & Conclusion: kaya naman, samakatuwid

To draw out a consequence or wrap up a point:

ConnectorMeaningExample
kaya namanand so / that is whyMahal ang bilihin; kaya naman nagtitipid ang marami. — Goods are dear; and so many economize.
samakatuwidtherefore / in other wordsSamakatuwid, tama ang unang palagay. — Therefore, the first view was right.
sa gayonthus / in that way…sa gayon, umunlad ang bayan. — …thus, the town prospered.

4. Concession: sa kabila ng, gayunpaman, bagama't

This is the argumentative power move: acknowledge an opposing fact, then push past it. Concession words are what make writing sound reasoned rather than one-sided:

ConnectorMeaningExample
sa kabila ngdespite / in spite of (+ noun)Sa kabila ng hirap, nagtagumpay sila. — Despite the hardship, they succeeded.
gayunpamannevertheless / howeverMahirap ito. Gayunpaman, susubukan natin. — This is hard. Nevertheless, we'll try.
bagama'talthough (bagaman + at)Bagama't pagod, tumulong pa rin siya. — Although tired, she still helped.
Concede to persuade. Starting with sa kabila ng or bagama't shows you've weighed the other side — which makes your conclusion land harder. "Bagama't may bisa ang plano, may panganib din" ("Although the plan is effective, there's also risk") sounds far more credible than ignoring the downside. You'll use this constantly in Lesson 19's arguments.

5. Building an Argument Paragraph

String cause → concession → conclusion and you've written a paragraph that reasons:

Dahil sa mataas na presyo…"] --> K["Concession

"Dahil sa pagtaas ng presyo ng bilihin, naghihirap ang maraming pamilya. Sa kabila ng mga hamong ito, marami pa rin ang nagtitiwala sa kinabukasan. Gayunpaman, kailangan ng tulong mula sa pamahalaan. Samakatuwid, dapat kumilos tayo nang sama-sama."

"Because of rising prices, many families are struggling. Despite these challenges, many still trust in the future. Nevertheless, help from the government is needed. Therefore, we must act together."

Practice — Connect an Argument

Exercise 1: Name the relationship

Answer cause, result, or concession:

  • dahil sa → _____ (Answer: cause)
  • samakatuwid → _____ (Answer: result)
  • sa kabila ng → _____ (Answer: concession)
  • gayunpaman → _____ (Answer: concession)

Exercise 2: Formal cause connector

Fill in dahil sa, dahil dito, or bunga ng:

  • _____ ulan, naantala ang biyahe. (because of + noun) → (Answer: dahil sa)
  • Mataas ang presyo; _____, nagtitipid kami. (because of this) → (Answer: dahil dito)
  • _____ polusyon ang sakit. (as a result of) → (Answer: bunga ng|dulot ng)

Exercise 3: Draw the conclusion

Fill in samakatuwid (therefore) or kaya naman (and so):

  • Mahal ang bilihin; _____ nagtitipid ang marami. (and so) → (Answer: kaya naman)
  • _____, tama ang unang palagay. (therefore) → (Answer: samakatuwid)

Exercise 4: Concede the point

Fill in sa kabila ng, gayunpaman, or bagama't:

  • _____ hirap, nagtagumpay sila. (despite + noun) → (Answer: sa kabila ng)
  • Mahirap ito. _____, susubukan natin. (nevertheless) → (Answer: gayunpaman)
  • _____ pagod, tumulong pa rin siya. (although) → (Answer: bagama't|bagaman)

Exercise 5: Casual → formal

Give the formal equivalent of the casual connector:

  • kasi (because) → formal: _____ (Answer: dahil sa|dahil|dahil dito|bunga ng)
  • pero (but) → formal contrast: _____ (Answer: gayunpaman|ngunit|subalit)

Where This Takes You

You've upgraded from kasi/kaya/pero to the connectors of real discourse: dahil sa / bunga ng for cause, samakatuwid / kaya naman for conclusions, and — the persuasive key — sa kabila ng / gayunpaman / bagama't for concession. These are the joints of an argument. Next comes a change of pace before we argue: idioms & figurative language.
