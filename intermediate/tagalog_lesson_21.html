Pormal na Pagsulat — Formal Writing: Letters & Email

Structure, salutations, and a respectful voice for letters and email

Everything at the formal end of the register dial (Lesson 13) — deep vocabulary, po/kayo, connectors like ngunit and samakatuwid — comes together when you write. This lesson gives you the skeleton of a Tagalog formal letter and email: the parts, the salutations, the openings and closings, and the respectful voice that ties them together.

1. When to Write Formally

Reach for formal Tagalog when writing to officials, elders, institutions, or strangers in an official capacity — applications, requests, complaints, invitations, thank-you letters. Signals: full sentences, po/opo, kayo/ninyo, formal vocabulary, and no Taglish.

2. Parts of a Formal Letter

A Tagalog formal letter follows the familiar shape, with Tagalog labels:

PartTagalog
DatePetsa
SalutationBating panimula (e.g., Mahal kong…, Kagalang-galang na…)
BodyKatawan ng liham
ClosingBating pangwakas (e.g., Gumagalang,)
SignatureLagda

3. Salutations & Titles

Open with a greeting sized to the relationship:

SalutationUse
Mahal kong [pangalan],Dear… (warm, personal)
Kagalang-galang na [Ginoo/Ginang],Respected/Honorable… (formal)
Ginoo / Binibini / Ginang,Mr. / Miss / Mrs. (Sir/Madam)

Titles: Ginoo (G.) = Mr., Binibini (Bb.) = Miss, Ginang (Gng.) = Mrs. — the same set from Lesson 13.

4. Openings & Closings

Set phrases carry the formal frame:

Opening (panimula)Closing (pangwakas)
Lumiliham ako upang… (I am writing in order to…)Gumagalang, (Respectfully,)
Sumusulat po ako kaugnay ng… (I write regarding…)Lubos na gumagalang, (Very respectfully yours,)
Nais ko pong ipahayag na… (I wish to express that…)Sumasainyo, (Yours,)
Gumagalang = "respecting." The standard sign-off Gumagalang, literally means "(the one) respecting" — the writer positions themselves as showing respect. Adding Lubos na ("wholly") intensifies it. These are the Tagalog counterparts of "Sincerely" / "Respectfully yours."

5. Email Conventions

Email keeps the formal voice but adds a few web-era pieces:

  • Paksa: — the Subject line (keep it short and clear).
  • Greeting: Magandang araw po, (Good day,) is a safe, warm-formal opener.
  • Body: still full sentences, still po/kayo; slightly less rigid than a paper letter.
  • Close: Maraming salamat po. Gumagalang, + your name.

6. A Model Letter

Ika-20 ng Setyembre, 2026

Kagalang-galang na Ginoong Santos,

Lumiliham po ako upang magpahayag ng interes sa bakanteng posisyon sa inyong kompanya. Naniniwala po akong angkop ang aking karanasan sa inyong pangangailangan.

Umaasa po akong mabigyan ng pagkakataong makapanayam. Maraming salamat po sa inyong panahon.

Gumagalang,
Maria de la Cruz

"September 20, 2026 — Respected Mr. Santos, I am writing to express interest in the vacant position at your company. I believe my experience fits your needs. I hope to be given the chance for an interview. Thank you very much for your time. Respectfully, Maria de la Cruz."

Practice — Write It Right

Exercise 1: Name the part

Answer salutation, closing, or date:

  • Gumagalang, → _____ (Answer: closing)
  • Kagalang-galang na Ginoo, → _____ (Answer: salutation)
  • Petsa → _____ (Answer: date)

Exercise 2: The right title

Give the Tagalog title:

  • Mr. → _____ (Answer: Ginoo|G.)
  • Miss → _____ (Answer: Binibini|Bb.)
  • Mrs. → _____ (Answer: Ginang|Gng.)

Exercise 3: Formal opening

Fill in the opening phrase for "I am writing in order to…":

  • _____ ako upang magpahayag ng interes. (I am writing) → (Answer: lumiliham)

Exercise 4: Close the letter

Fill in the sign-off:

  • _____ (Respectfully,) → (Answer: gumagalang)
  • _____ na gumagalang, (Very respectfully — one word) → (Answer: lubos)

Exercise 5: Email pieces

Fill in the Tagalog word/phrase:

  • _____: (the Subject line label) → (Answer: paksa)
  • Magandang _____ po, (Good day — greeting) → (Answer: araw)

Where This Takes You

You can now assemble a Tagalog formal letter or email: petsa, a salutation (Kagalang-galang na Ginoo,), a formal opening (Lumiliham ako upang…), a body in respectful register, and a closing (Gumagalang,) over your lagda — plus email's Paksa: and Magandang araw po.
