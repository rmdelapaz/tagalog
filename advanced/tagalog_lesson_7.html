Akademikong Filipino

Defining, citing, hedging — and the shape of a sanaysay

Filipino is a full academic language. Universities in the Philippines produce theses, journals, and textbooks in it, and the KWF publishes terminology for it. Academic Filipino is not everyday Filipino with longer words — it has its own sentence shape, its own framing verbs, and a strong preference for hedging over asserting.

Everything you have learned so far converges here: the ay-inversion of Lesson 3, the nested clauses of Lesson 4, the hedges of Lesson 5, and the native vocabulary of Lesson 6.

1. The Shape of a Paper

SectionEnglishWhat goes in it
BuodAbstractThe whole paper in one paragraph
PanimulaIntroductionBackground and the problem
LayuninObjectivesWhat the study aims to do
MetodolohiyaMethodologyHow it was done
Datos at ResultaData and resultsWhat was found
PagsusuriAnalysisWhat the findings mean
PagtalakayDiscussionHow they fit the wider picture
KongklusyonConclusionThe answer, and its limits
SanggunianReferencesSources cited
Buod vs lagom. A buod is the abstract that opens a paper. A lagom is a summary that closes a section or a discussion. Both translate as "summary" in English; they are not interchangeable in Filipino.

2. Defining a Term

Academic writing defines before it argues. The standard frames:

FrameExample
Ang X ay tinutukoy bilang…Ang kultura ay tinutukoy bilang kabuuan ng mga kaugalian ng isang lipunan.
Tinutukoy ng X ang…Tinutukoy ng terminong ito ang proseso ng pagbabago.
Sa pag-aaral na ito, ang X ay nangangahulugang…Sa pag-aaral na ito, ang "kabataan" ay nangangahulugang 15 hanggang 24 taong gulang.
Ang X ay binubuo ng…Ang wika ay binubuo ng tunog, salita, at kahulugan.

Notice that every one of them is an ay-inversion or a fronted object-focus verb. That is not decoration — a definition needs its term stated first, which is exactly what inversion does.

3. Citing a Source

Filipino citation is front-loaded: the source is named before the claim, not after it.

Ayon kay Santos (2019), tumataas ang bilang ng mag-aaral taon-taon.

Batay sa datos ng PSA, bumaba ang antas ng kahirapan.

Ayon sa pag-aaral nina Reyes at Dela Cruz, may ugnayan ang dalawang salik.

Sinabi ni Rizal na ang kabataan ang pag-asa ng bayan.

Ayon kay takes a person; ayon sa takes anything else (a study, a report, an agency). Nina is the plural of ni — use it for two or more named authors.

4. Hedging — the Register's Defining Habit

Academic Filipino under-claims on principle. A flat assertion reads as unscholarly; the hedges of Lesson 5 do the work.

HedgeEnglishStrength
Maaaring sabihin na…It may be said that…cautious
Tila ipinapakita ng datos na…The data seem to show that…cautious
Nagmumungkahi ito na…This suggests that…moderate
Malinaw na ipinapakita na…It clearly shows that…strong
Nangangailangan pa ng karagdagang pag-aaralFurther study is neededthe standard closing caveat

❌ Ang social media ang dahilan ng problema. — flat, overclaiming.

✅ Nagmumungkahi ang datos na may ugnayan ang paggamit ng social media at ang naturang problema. — the same idea, scholarly.

5. Presenting Data, and Switching to Plain Language

FrameEnglish
Ipinapakita ng datos na…The data show that…
Lumalabas sa resulta na…It emerges from the results that…
Makikita sa Talahanayan 1 na…It can be seen in Table 1 that…
Sa madaling salita…In short / put plainly…

Sa madaling salita is the register's own plain-language switch — the signal that the writer is about to restate a dense point simply. Use it after your hardest paragraph, not before it.

6. A Worked Paragraph

"Ang wika ay tinutukoy sa pag-aaral na ito bilang sistema ng komunikasyon na ginagamit ng isang pamayanan. Ayon kay Santos (2019), mahigpit ang ugnayan ng wika at pagkakakilanlan. Ipinapakita ng datos na mas mataas ang partisipasyon ng mga mag-aaral kapag ginagamit ang unang wika sa silid-aralan. Maaaring sabihin na nakatutulong ang unang wika sa pagkatuto, bagaman nangangailangan pa ng karagdagang pag-aaral upang matiyak ito."

Four moves: define (tinutukoy bilang) → cite (Ayon kay) → present data (Ipinapakita ng datos) → hedge the conclusion (Maaaring sabihin… bagaman nangangailangan pa). That is a whole academic paragraph in four sentences.

Practice — Writing Like a Scholar

Exercise 1: Name the section

Give the Filipino heading:

  • Introduction → _____ (Answer: panimula)
  • Methodology → _____ (Answer: metodolohiya)
  • Analysis → _____ (Answer: pagsusuri)
  • References → _____ (Answer: sanggunian)

Exercise 2: Buod or lagom?

  • the abstract at the front of a paper → _____ (Answer: buod)
  • a summary closing a discussion → _____ (Answer: lagom)

Exercise 3: Cite it correctly

Fill in ayon kay, ayon sa, or batay sa:

  • _____ Santos (2019), tumataas ang bilang. (a person) → (Answer: ayon kay)
  • _____ pag-aaral, may ugnayan ang dalawa. (a study) → (Answer: ayon sa)
  • _____ datos ng PSA, bumaba ang kahirapan. (data) → (Answer: batay sa)

Exercise 4: Hedge the claim

Complete the frame:

  • _____ sabihin na nakatutulong ito. (it may be said) → (Answer: maaaring)
  • _____ ito na may ugnayan ang dalawa. (this suggests) → (Answer: nagmumungkahi)
  • Nangangailangan pa ng karagdagang _____. (study) → (Answer: pag-aaral)

Exercise 5: Define the term

  • Ang kultura ay _____ bilang kabuuan ng mga kaugalian. (is referred to) → (Answer: tinutukoy)
  • Ang wika ay _____ ng tunog, salita, at kahulugan. (is composed) → (Answer: binubuo)
  • Sa _____ salita, mas madali itong matutuhan. (in short) → (Answer: madaling)

Where This Takes You

Academic Filipino is careful, hedged, and slow. The next register is its opposite in temperament and its twin in structure: law and news, where the same inverted, object-focus sentences are compressed to the bone and the actor often disappears entirely.
